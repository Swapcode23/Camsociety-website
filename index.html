About CAM Society

A creative group of undergraduates learning, improving, and applying software, manufacturing, and technical skills to solve real-world problems.

⚙

CAD/CAM/CAE Skills

Integrating design and manufacturing skills with advanced automation through hands-on learning and workshops.

🎓

Beyond Curriculum

Teaching manufacturing technology that goes beyond the scope of the classroom, preparing students for real-world challenges.

💡

Frugal Innovation

Focusing on sustainable, low-cost product development in biomedical, automobile, agriculture, and household sectors.
