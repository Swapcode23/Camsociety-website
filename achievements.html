Achievements

Milestones and recognition earned by CAM Society and its members.

2024

Gold Medal at VIKRAM AWARD 2024

Team CAM Society (HLV Sai Krishna, Ritik Jangid, Vaishnav Pratap) secured a Gold Medal and ₹50,000 for the best project at the VIKRAM AWARD 2024, organized by Secure Meters Pvt. Ltd.

2019

Autodesk University 2019

CAM Society members participated in Autodesk University 2019, showcasing innovative projects and learning from industry leaders.

2018

FOMT 2018

Members participated and excelled at FOMT 2018, demonstrating advanced manufacturing skills and product design capabilities.

2017

Indo Asian Solar Challenge 2017

Team 'XCLERATE' won 2nd position in the Indo Asian Solar Challenge 2017, a prestigious national-level competition.

2017

Design for Audio Technology Challenge

CAM Society, in association with Autodesk Inc., organized a student competition where 25 designs were evaluated. Top 5 winners received cash prizes, goodies, and certificates.
